Core Insight:
Swiggy’s onboarding flow is visually polished but delays value delivery until Step 6. Users are asked for login, OTP, and location permissions before seeing restaurants, increasing activation friction and early abandonment risk.

Top Friction Points

Friction Why It Matters Business Risk
Login Before Value Users must share phone number before seeing restaurants. High onboarding abandonment.
Permission Fatigue Generic location popup appears before trust formation. Lower location opt-in rate.
Checkout Complexity Too many fields during highest-intent moment. Cart abandonment increase.

Improved PM Recommendations

1. Browse Before Login P0

Allow users to browse restaurants before OTP verification. Move authentication closer to checkout intent.

Expected Impact: Faster aha moment, reduced onboarding friction, improved activation rate.

2. Value-Framed Permission Prompt P0

Add a branded pre-permission screen explaining why location improves delivery speed and restaurant relevance.

Expected Impact: Higher location permission acceptance rate.

3. Simplified First Checkout P1

Enable auto-filled addresses and default Cash on Delivery for first-time users.

Expected Impact: Reduced first-order checkout abandonment.

Product Management Takeaway

The highest-performing onboarding systems reduce effort until users experience clear product value. Swiggy currently prioritizes data collection before value realization, creating unnecessary activation friction in the earliest stages of the funnel.
